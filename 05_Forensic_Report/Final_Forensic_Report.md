\## 2. Executive Summary



This report documents a simulated ransomware incident investigation involving a fictional healthcare network.



The investigation covers first responder procedures, synthetic evidence collection, evidence integrity verification, timeline analysis, and safe malware behavior documentation.



All evidence was generated exclusively for this academic project. No real healthcare infrastructure or patient information was accessed.



\## 3. Investigation Objectives



1\. Document first responder procedures.

2\. Establish a structured evidence register.

3\. Preserve synthetic evidence and create working copies.

4\. Verify evidence integrity using SHA-256.

5\. Reconstruct the simulated incident timeline.

6\. Document simulated ransomware indicators.

7\. Prepare a structured forensic investigation report.

\## 5. Evidence Integrity Verification



SHA-256 hashes were calculated for all three original evidence files and their corresponding working copies.



\*\*Verification Results:\*\*

\- Login\_Records.csv: Match

\- Ransom\_Note.txt: Match

\- System\_Logs.txt: Match



All three working copies produced hashes identical to their original synthetic files.
